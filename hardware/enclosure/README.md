# Enclosure

3D models for the printed enclosure (STEP + STL) go here. Not added yet.

Things the design has to allow for:

- A window for the 4.2" e-paper and a way to hold the module flat.
- A thin wall (about 1–2 mm) over each of the three TTP223 touch pads.
- The solar panel on a face that gets light, with a hole for its wires.
- An opening for the ESP32-C3's USB-C connector, for programming.
- An opening for SW1.
- A small hole above the INMP441 microphone port.
- Room for the battery, the perfboard and the charger and protection modules.
